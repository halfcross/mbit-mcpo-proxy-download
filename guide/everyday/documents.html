Documents (local search) ​

If your build shows Documents in the sidebar, you added the Local RAG style preset from the Library. It keeps search on your Mac for files you allow.

Typical flow ​

  1. Add the preset from Connections → Library (look for the private search / Local RAG entry).
  2. Complete any folder prompts so the app knows where documents live.
  3. Open Documents in the sidebar to list, check status, or ingest files—plain buttons, no terminal required for daily use.
  4. Keep the assistant bridge on under Home when an assistant should query this connection.

Privacy ​

Indexing and search run through the connection you configured. Do not point it at folders you are not allowed to index.

Related ​

  • Connections — enable or disable the connection.
  • Home — copy the link your assistant uses.
