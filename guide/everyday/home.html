Home ​

Home is where you turn the assistant bridge on or off and where you copy links for other apps.

What the subtitle means ​

Turn on the assistant bridge, then use your connections in ChatGPT, Open WebUI, or other assistants.

The bridge is a small local service. When it runs, your enabled connections each get a stable URL on your Mac (by default on port 8000).

Status strip (three steps) ​

Read the strip from left to right:

StepPlain meaning
Built-in engineThe packaged runtime is available.
BridgeThe assistant bridge is stopped, starting, or running.
ConnectionsHow many connections are switched on versus how many you have saved.

The pill in the top-right corner summarises the same story (Stopped, Running, and so on). The Assistant bridge card sits directly under the strip in this view: use Turn on / Turn off there, and Apply changes after you edited connections (it restarts the bridge so new settings apply).

Before you start (first-time hints) ​

If the engine is missing or a port is busy, you may see a short Before you start checklist on Home with items such as the built-in engine and port availability. Expand Details for troubleshooting if the app is still working through a problem. (That checklist is not shown in the screenshot above—open the app after a fresh install to see it when prerequisites fail.)

Links for other apps ​

Once the bridge has run at least once, you get a table of enabled connections.

  • Copy link — paste into the assistant.
  • Reference — optional technical page.

If nothing appears here, open Connections and make sure at least one connection is enabled, then use Apply changes on Home.

Related pages ​

  • Connections — Library and My connections.
  • Settings — change the HTTP port or advanced options.
  • Open WebUI — automatic sync instead of manual links.
