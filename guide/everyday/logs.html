On this page

Logs ​

Logs shows recent output from the local assistant bridge (the bundled mcpo process on your computer)—startup lines, connection successes, and errors. It is not a Docker container log; the bridge runs as a normal app process.

Empty state ​

If you just opened Logs and see a short message, start (or restart) the bridge from Home, then open Logs again.

Reading the stream ​

  • New lines appear at the bottom while you stay scrolled to the end.
  • Scroll up to pause auto-follow so you can read older lines.

What to look for ​

  • Successfully connected — good sign after startup.
  • address already in use — another program took the HTTP port; change the port under Settings or quit the other program.
  • OAuth — sign-in happens in your default browser; follow prompts there.
